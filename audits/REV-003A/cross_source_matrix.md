## Deliberate combined Tier 1 synthesis

Read together with exact `SRC-0004`, `SRC-0007`, `SRC-0021`, and separate proxy/companion `SRC-0036`, the Tier 1 corpus establishes substantial prior coverage of: actor-indexed own/other commitment state; ambiguous candidate readings and corrections; public/common commitment and acknowledgement-conditioned uptake; multimodal evidence banks and closure; qualitative uncertainty and action-sensitive clarification; traceable pre-execution gating; situated physical consequences; and empirical common-ground tracking.

The combined audit therefore prohibits presenting any of those mechanisms alone as a PCC novelty. Within the exact versions reviewed, no single source was located that jointly implements an inspectable diagnostic which compares explicit action-sensitive candidate values, judges evidence sufficiency under formal organizational role/authority and durable artifact/workflow constraints, can abstain, exposes evidence and consequences to participants, supports challenge of the diagnostic decision, and directly evaluates correctness, calibration, and contestability. This is a bounded audit residual, not a priority finding. `NOV-0001` remains `candidate`.

`SRC-0036` remains separate proxy/companion context; no finding is transferred between it and exact `SRC-0004`. Exact-source/version boundaries in every source card remain controlling.
