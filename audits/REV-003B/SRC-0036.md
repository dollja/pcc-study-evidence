## D. Boundary verdict

1. **Demonstrated mechanism:** a formal, unimplemented constraint-based model of partial information, intentional states, actor/context/time-indexed commitment, imperative/declarative updates, acceptance, and commitment-to-action policies.
2. **PCC coverage:** substantial formal overlap with PCC-C02, PCC-C03, and PCC-C05; partial overlap with PCC-C04 and PCC-C06.
3. **Unlocated or excluded:** evidence-sufficiency promotion, organizational authority, persistent work artifacts/workflow, repair/retraction/reversal, implemented inspectable decisions, and human contestation. Interpretation itself is explicitly outside the current model.
4. **Necessity to PCC:** public evidential warrant, governed operational state, and contestability remain necessary links in PCC's proposed contribution even though actor-indexed mutual commitment and action constraints are inherited comparators.
5. **Verdict:** **partial overlap (high confidence for this 2000 text)**. It is a strong formal comparator for actor-indexed/nested commitment and commitment-to-action constraints, but is not implemented or evaluated and omits governance and contestation. It is **SRC-0036 only**, not evidence about the exact 1997 SRC-0004.
