- **Source type:** formal dialogue-state model with reported use of substantial parts in the DenK artificial-assistant system; this paper does not report a controlled empirical evaluation.
- **Task setting and actors:** two interlocutors coordinate linguistic and non-linguistic action during cooperative activity. The worked multimodal case uses a shared visible block workspace in which one participant can manipulate blocks and the other can point and acts as task expert.
- **Target unit:** actor-associated dialogue state, public and shared commitments, unsettled judgments, conditionally relevant responses, and commitment-constrained linguistic or physical action.
- **Success criterion:** the model should derive contextually appropriate responses and explain how public signals, acceptance, denial, and physical action update commitments and constrain what participants do next.

## D. Boundary verdict

1. **Demonstrated or formally specified mechanism:** an actor-associated dialogue-state model with own/other commitment attribution, public and shared commitment sets, unsettled judgments, explicit Transparency and Discussion update rules, default cancellation, multimodal evidence, and commitment-constrained physical action. The paper reports use of substantial parts in DenK but does not evaluate them here.
2. **PCC components covered:** strong overlap with `PCC-C02`, `PCC-C04`, `PCC-C05`, and `PCC-C06`; partial overlap with bounded second-order attribution in `PCC-C03` and situated evidence/observability in `PCC-C08`.
3. **Residual links not located after scoped review:** explicit extraction and comparison of competing action-sensitive task-variable interpretations; calibrated evidence-sufficiency or abstention; formal organizational authority/permission; durable work-artifact and workflow-governed promotion; participant-facing diagnostic evidence and rationale; challenge or appeal of the system's promotion decision; and empirical evaluation of diagnosis, calibration, or contestability.
4. **Why the residual matters to PCC:** the source substantially weakens any claim that actor-indexed commitment state, public/shared uptake, or commitment-to-action transition is new. PCC's defensible residual must instead concern an inspectable diagnostic decision over whether publicly available evidence, role/authority, and artifact/workflow state are sufficient to promote one of several materially different interpretations into action-guiding coordination commitment.
5. **Verdict:** **possible near-equivalent, high confidence for the exact 1997 text, but not novelty-defeating on the current mechanism definition.** It is the strongest REV-003B comparator for situated public/shared commitment updates connected to consequential action. It does not jointly supply PCC's explicit action-sensitive candidate comparison, organizational governance, participant-facing contestability, and evaluation target. `NOV-0001` therefore remains `candidate`, and the combined Tier 1 conclusion still requires REV-003A and deliberate cross-source synthesis.
