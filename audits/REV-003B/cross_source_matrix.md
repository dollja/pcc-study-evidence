## Cross-source boundary

The exact Piwek 1997 audit materially changes the REV-003B boundary. `SRC-0004` is the strongest source in this half of the audit for actor-associated own/other commitment state, explicit public/shared commitments, public-uptake rules, multimodal evidence, and commitment-constrained consequential action. Those mechanisms are prior work and must not be claimed as PCC novelties.

Within the audited REV-003B exact texts, the remaining joint gap is narrower: no one reviewed source combines explicit action-sensitive candidate-value comparison; calibrated evidence-sufficiency and abstention; formal organizational authority and durable workflow-artifact governance; a participant-facing diagnostic record that exposes evidence, alternatives, warrant, and consequences; a path to challenge that diagnostic; and direct evaluation of diagnostic correctness, calibration, and contestability. `SRC-0007` supplies uncertainty/cost-sensitive ask-versus-act behavior, `SRC-0021` supplies traceable-evidence pre-execution gating, and `SRC-0004` supplies the closest situated public/shared commitment-to-action mechanism.

This is still not a final novelty conclusion. REV-003A and a deliberate combined Tier 1 synthesis remain outstanding, `SRC-0036` remains separate proxy/companion context, and `NOV-0001` remains `candidate`.
