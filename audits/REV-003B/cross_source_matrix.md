## Cross-source boundary

SRC-0007 supplies the clearest uncertainty/cost-sensitive ask-versus-act inheritance. SRC-0021 supplies the closest implemented traceable-evidence, pre-execution action gate. SRC-0036 supplies formal actor-indexed and nested mutual-commitment contexts plus commitment-to-action constraints. No audited REV-003B exact text jointly supplies multi-actor public-state promotion, organizational role/authority and durable work-artifact governance, and participant-facing contestation. That bounded residual is not a final novelty conclusion: SRC-0004 remains not assessable, REV-003A is outstanding, and `NOV-0001` remains candidate.
